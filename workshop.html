Call for Workshop Proposals – IEEE MOST 2027

Workshop Date: April 4, 2027
Location: Cal Poly Pomona, Pomona, California
Workshop Proposal Deadline: November 25, 2026

Overview

IEEE MOST 2027 invites proposals for half-day or full-day workshops to be held on April 4, 2027, in conjunction with the conference at Cal Poly Pomona.

IEEE MOST is the premier forum for advancing mobility research across land, air, and sea domains. The workshop program aims to foster interactive discussions, present early-stage interdisciplinary research, and explore emerging technological challenges at the intersection of computing, intelligent transportation, physical intelligence, and autonomous operations.

Topics of Interest

We solicit workshop proposals covering specialized, emerging, or cross-disciplinary topics complementary to the main IEEE MOST tracks. Suggested topics include, but are not limited to:

  • Physical Intelligence & Embodied AI: Physical AI systems, embodied agents, robotics, multimodal perception, and autonomous physical interactions.
  • Connected & Autonomous Systems: V2X communications, dynamic path planning, cooperative perception, and fleet coordination across land, air, and sea.
  • AI & Edge/Cloud Computing: On-device learning, federated intelligence, real-time edge analytics, and cloud orchestration for mobility platforms.
  • Smart Infrastructure & Urban Analytics: Intelligent Traffic Management Systems (ITMS), dynamic logistics, public transit optimization, micro-mobility, and Mobility-as-a-Service (MaaS).
  • Cybersecurity, Safety & Reliability: Resilient vehicular architectures, privacy-preserving location services, fault diagnosis, and safety verification.
  • Sustainability & Zero-Emission Mobility: EV grid integration, battery intelligence, energy-efficient routing, and smart charging infrastructure.
  • Digital Twins & Simulation: Real-time digital twins for traffic modeling, spatial computing, and hardware-in-the-loop simulation environments.

Submission Requirements

Workshop proposals must be submitted as a single PDF (maximum 4 pages in standard IEEE format) containing the following information:

1. Basic Information

  • Workshop Title & Acronym
  • Duration: Half-day (approximately 3.5 hours) or full-day (approximately 7 hours).
  • Organizers: Names, affiliations, contact email addresses, and brief bios (100–150 words each) of all co-chairs.

2. Scope & Rationale

  • Abstract: A 150–200 word summary of the workshop.
  • Motivation: Explain why the workshop is timely and how it aligns with and complements IEEE MOST 2027.
  • Topics of Interest: List the specific research areas covered by the workshop.

3. Execution & Program Plan

  • Session Structure: Proposed format, such as peer-reviewed paper presentations, keynote talks, lightning talks, or interactive panel discussions.
  • Review Process: Plan for peer review (minimum 3 reviews per paper) and conflict-of-interest management.
  • Program Committee: Tentative list of prospective Technical Program Committee (TPC) members.

Organizer Responsibilities

Organizers of accepted workshops are expected to:

  1. Formally issue and distribute the workshop Call for Papers (CFP).
  2. Manage paper submissions and peer reviews through the designated submission system.
  3. Adhere strictly to the main conference timeline for notification and camera-ready submission.
  4. Direct and chair the workshop sessions on-site during IEEE MOST 2027.

All accepted and presented workshop papers will be published in the IEEE MOST 2027 Conference Proceedings and submitted to IEEE Xplore.
