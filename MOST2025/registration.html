Author registration requirements

1. At least one author per conference paper must be registered at the FULL main conference rate (i.e., IEEE Member / Non-Member registration), regardless of whether or not they are students.

FULL registration refers to non-student registration. Therefore, if you are a student author, please opt for FULL registration (i.e., IEEE Member / Non-Member registration) rather than student registration . This is unless another author has already completed the FULL registration for the same paper.

2. The author registration deadline is April 1, 2025.

Each author registration is valid for ONE paper.

There is an additional pages fee of $100. A registrant can add up to 2 additional pages for each paper.

For questions regarding paper submissions, please contact the Publication Chair Dr. Lanyu Xu at lxu@oakland.edu.

Cancellation Policy

All refund/cancellation requests must be received in writing to the Financial Chair Dr. Jiang Li at jli@howard.edu and CC the Registration Chair Dr. Sidi Lu at sidi@wm.edu by April 25, 2025, 11:59 PM AoE Time. There will be an administrative fee of $50 for cancelled registrations. You may only cancel your registration before the cancellation deadline. Author registration is not refundable.At least one author per paper must be registered at the FULL main conference rate (i.e., IEEE Member / Non-Member registration), regardless of whether or not they are students.
