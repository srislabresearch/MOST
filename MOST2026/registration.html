Early registration / author deadline

Author: March 25, 2026
Early Bird: April 01, 2026

Author registration requirements

1. At least one author per paper must be registered at the FULL rate (i.e., IEEE Member / Non-Member registration), regardless of whether or not they are students.

FULL registration refers to non-student registration. Therefore, if you are a student author, please opt for FULL registration (i.e., IEEE Member / Non-Member registration) rather than student registration. This is unless another author has already completed the FULL registration for the same paper.

2. The author registration deadline is March 25, 2026.

Each author registration is valid for ONE paper.

There is an additional pages fee of $100. A registrant can add up to 2 additional pages for each paper.

For questions regarding paper submissions, please contact the Publication Chair Dr. Liangkai Liu at liangkai@umich.edu.

Cancellation Policy

Author registration is NOT refundable. All other refund/cancellation requests must be received in writing to ieeecs-reg+MOST@computer.org and CC the Registration Chair Dr. Sidi Lu at sidi@wm.edu by April 4, 2026, 11:59 PM (EST time). There will be an administrative fee of US$50 for canceled registrations from each cancellation.

VISA Support

If you require a visa invitation letter, one will be automatically generated using your name, organization, and address after you complete the conference registration. If you need a customized letter, you may request one that includes additional information, such as your date of birth or passport details (number, date of issue, expiration date, and place of issue). Please email your request to the MOST 2026 General Co-Chair: Caisheng Wang at cwang@wayne.edu.
