Conference Venue

IEEE MOST 2026 will take place in person in the vibrant city of Detroit - the Motor City that put the world on wheels and continues to drive innovation across industry, technology, and culture. The conference will be hosted on the campus of Wayne State University at St. Andrew’s Hall, located at 5105 Anthony Wayne Dr, Detroit, MI 48202. Let’s immerse ourselves in this historic venue while exploring the most cutting-edge research on smart mobility.

Travel: Detroit Metropolitan Wayne County Airport

Detroit Metropolitan Wayne County Airport (DTW) is the primary international gateway serving the Detroit metropolitan region and one of the busiest air transportation hubs in the United States. Located in Romulus, Michigan, approximately 20–22 miles southwest of Wayne State University in Midtown Detroit, the airport typically requires about 25–35 minutes of travel by car under normal traffic conditions, providing convenient access for visiting scholars, students, and conference attendees. DTW serves as a major hub for Delta Air Lines and offers extensive domestic and international connections across North America, Europe, and Asia. Featuring two main passenger terminals, McNamara and Evans, with modern amenities, efficient ground transportation options, and strong winter operational reliability, DTW plays a vital role in supporting both the regional economy and the academic and research activities centered in Detroit.
